# Organize evidence

Input: collected sources and any user-provided outline. Output: 素材整理（按论点）.md and evidence-map.json.

For each source, record its distinct contribution, factual claims, attributed opinions, relevant passages, limitations, and possible claim group. Separate direct quotations from paraphrases. Preserve uncertainty, conditions, units, dates, and attribution. Do not invent missing identities or infer viewpoint from geography.

Group sources around evidence-supported claims rather than a list of publishers. Follow an existing approved outline when suitable. Record disagreements and evidence that does not fit the current structure. Syndicated copies are not independent corroboration.

For each claim, list supporting URLs and limitations in evidence-map.json. Evidence requirements depend on the task; mark weak support and propose supplementation rather than fabricating confidence. Present material outline changes to the user when a decision is needed.

For incremental work, compare source URLs with existing entries, process additions, and update affected claims. Do not silently discard earlier evidence. Hand off organized text, the evidence map, and open questions.
